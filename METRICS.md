# North Star Metric

Completed audits per week.

This metric directly measures product usefulness and top-of-funnel lead generation.

---

# Input Metrics

## 1. Audit Completion Rate

Measures usability and onboarding quality.

## 2. Share Rate

Measures virality and product-market resonance.

## 3. Consultation Conversion Rate

Measures monetization effectiveness.

---

# First Instrumentation

I would instrument:

* form abandonment
* tool selection frequency
* audit completion funnel
* CTA click-through rate
* share button usage

---

# Pivot Threshold

If fewer than 10% of completed audits lead to consultation requests after significant traffic volume, the positioning or monetization model likely needs adjustment.
