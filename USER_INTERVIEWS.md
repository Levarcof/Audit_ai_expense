# Interview 1

## A.K — Technical Founder — Seed Startup

### Quotes

* “We buy AI subscriptions impulsively.”
* “Nobody tracks whether the plans still make sense.”
* “I would absolutely share a savings report with my cofounder.”

### Most Surprising Insight

The founder cared more about visibility than raw savings.

### Design Impact

I prioritized clean shareable reports and transparency.

# Interview 2

## R.M — Engineering Lead — SaaS Startup

### Quotes

* “Enterprise plans are often unnecessary.”
* “We forget to remove inactive seats.”
* “I need explainable recommendations.”

### Most Surprising Insight

The engineering lead distrusted fully AI-generated financial advice.

### Design Impact

I switched to deterministic audit logic.

### Quotes
